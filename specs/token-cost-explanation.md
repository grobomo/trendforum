### 2. What Happened (2-3 paragraphs)
- AI assistant (Coconut) monitors Microsoft Teams for Joel's sales squad
- The Teams integration used a *polling* approach — checking for new messages every 3 minutes
- Each poll woke the primary AI model (Claude Opus, the most expensive tier) with full conversation context
- When multiple chats had activity, the context window filled up and "compacted" — re-sending the entire 200K token context
- One session compacted 42 times, each time re-sending ~200K tokens at $5/M = catastrophic cost spiral
- During this, the assistant was unresponsive because context kept filling and resetting

### 3. Why It Took Time to Fix
- Joel was the only human operator and the system was unresponsive during the spike
- The polling was a known interim solution while webhooks were being built
- Once identified, polling was killed immediately and permanently

### 4. Measures to Prevent Recurrence
- **Polling permanently removed** — all scripts archived, crons deleted, service disabled
- **Webhook-only architecture** — Microsoft pushes notifications instead of us pulling
- **Haiku preprocessor** — cheap model ($0.80/M vs $5/M) triages messages before waking the expensive model
- **Cost monitoring** — automated hourly token usage reports with alerts on cost spikes
- **Session compaction limits** — flag sessions exceeding 10 compactions as runaway
- **Estimated new cost: ~$X/hr** (to be filled after monitoring period)
